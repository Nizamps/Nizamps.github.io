Starting from zero

The pilot batch that built SalesOps from scratch.

When I joined Even Healthcare, there was no SalesOps function — we were the pilot batch and we started everything from the ground up. No reports, no dashboards, no visibility into what the floor or the field was actually doing.

I built the input-metric layer first: hourly calling reports, Good Lead marks, and Home Visits booked every half hour. Then the predictive layer on top — HV forecasts three days out, an expected-closure funnel for the next day, and daily activity funnels showing call connection rates and which leads were genuinely worked.

The thesis was simple: if you can see the inputs cleanly, you can forecast the revenue. Everything that followed — field tracking, dashboards, the auto-dialer, the live HV report — was built to make that loop tighter.

Quota setting

Tenure target and incentive
A quota is a commitment on what a person can deliver over a tenure. It works when the target sits next to the result every day and the incentive is paid on activity that has been verified.
  • Target vs revenue for every agent, with achievement bars and zone and POD-leader filters, so a shortfall is visible while it can still be fixed.
  • Compensation reporting for the whole field channel: RM Travel Allowance is reimbursed on verified visits, and payroll is computed on visits, leads and attendance.
  • Leaderboards that make targets and rank visible to the floor.
  • Weekly revenue trend per rep, so a target is read against pace and not only at month end.

Capacity planning

Human capacity
Capacity is how many leads and visits a team can genuinely work in a day. It depends on who is present, how long they talk, and how leads are shared out. Planning it means measuring those three things.
  • Attendance and punch-in by role and city, with present, no-punch and late flagged (205 agents in view).
  • Hourly calling and talk time by agent and slot, including the peak calling window of the day.
  • Ameyo auto-dialer brought in to raise the volume and consistency of contact.
  • Connection funnel that shows which leads agents actually worked, so effort and output can be compared.

Lead analysis

MQL, SQL, P0 and P1 leads
Not every lead deserves the same call. MQL is every lead that enters through a channel. SQL is the lead marked Good Lead or booked. P0 leads carry the highest intent and are worked first, P1 leads follow, and the rest is worked as capacity allows.
  • Good Lead marks captured hourly as the SQL signal.
  • Lead routing and assignment so P0 leads reach the right rep first.
  • Clinic attribution to see which sources feed the funnel.
  • Even Pay clinic network as a dedicated source of lead flow.
